# Employee Engagement, Satisfaction & Burnout Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes employee engagement, satisfaction, workplace conditions, and burnout indicators to understand workforce well-being and organizational patterns.

The analysis combines multiple HR factors such as job satisfaction, management support, recognition, career growth, workload, working hours, overtime, work-life balance, and team collaboration.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze employee engagement levels.
- Study employee satisfaction patterns.
- Identify burnout risk factors.
- Analyze department-wise engagement and burnout.
- Examine the impact of workload and working hours.
- Analyze management support and recognition.
- Identify employees showing higher burnout indicators.

## 🔍 Engagement & Burnout Analysis

Engagement and burnout scores are calculated using selected workplace factors.

Employees are categorized into different engagement and burnout levels to identify workforce patterns and potential areas requiring attention.
